The Founder

Built from service. Driven by purpose.

David Woods Jr. — Founder & Owner

Eminent Forensic Cleaning was established in 2021 by David Woods Jr. — but the story behind it began long before, in a life defined by service, responsibility, and a deep respect for people in their most difficult moments.

  1. Military · 2007–2008

    David served in the United States Army and deployed to Iraq, where he was awarded the Combat Action Badge. After returning to Virginia, he joined the Virginia Army National Guard.

  2. Law Enforcement

    He earned an Associate's Degree in Administration of Justice, then became a police officer serving in a specialized unit focused on investigations, warrants, and complex cases.

  3. Forensic Science

    David went on to work for the Department of Forensic Science as a Forensic Evidence Specialist — bringing a scientific, methodical approach to difficult scenes.

  4. Eminent Forensic Cleaning · 2021 → Present

    He founded Eminent Forensic Cleaning, completing his first job with help from his father. Today the company works alongside law enforcement, property management, insurance companies, social services, and funeral homes across Virginia.

Common Questions

Answers when you're not sure what to do.

Should I or someone else attempt to clean this?

We recommend you don't. Household cleaning products are not enough for this type of situation, which requires professional equipment, training, and proper decontamination.

Will my insurance policy cover your services?

Most insurance policies do cover our services, and we work directly with insurance companies. If that's not an option for you, we also offer flexible payment plans.

Does law enforcement clean crime scenes?

No. Once their investigation is completed, the scene is released back to the homeowner, family, property manager, or business owner — and that's where we come in.

Can biohazard exposure be a health risk?

Yes. Bloodborne pathogens can pose a risk to human health, which is why professional cleaning and disinfection is important.

Does this need to be cleaned right away?

Biohazards should be removed, cleaned, and disinfected as soon as possible. We're available 24/7 to respond quickly.
